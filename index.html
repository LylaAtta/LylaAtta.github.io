About Me

Hello! I am an MD-PhD candidate at Johns Hopkins University. I am interested in systems biology and data science and how computational and mathematical methods can be used to understand complexity in disease. I have completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering in the JEFworks lab. My PhD work focused on developing computational methods for spatial transcriptomics analysis to understand how cells are spatially organized within tissues and how this changes in disease settings. I am excited to build on this work by developing methods to combine high-dimensional molecular data with high-resolution physiologic data to understand the biological mechanisms driving clinical heterogenity in critical illness.
